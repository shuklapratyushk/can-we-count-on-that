\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Background.} Cancer-genomics frequency queries can leave patients versus specimens, assay denominators, specimen context, and mutation classes unresolved. Missing measurements introduce another uncertainty: every alternative must refer to the same unmeasured specimens. Independent identification intervals can compare incompatible worlds and falsely suggest that clarification is necessary.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Methods.} CLARA enumerates 16 typed scientific specifications and represents an unprofiled specimen as absent, broad-only, or restricted-and-broad. Sharing these nested states across queries and factoring contributions by patient gives sharp absolute discrepancies and parametric relative-discrepancy bounds. Exact rational count-support comparisons decide the strict 10\% relative OR 5-percentage-point (pp) cutoff. A violating-pair graph supports sufficient stopping and expected-cost or minimax clarification. A separate point guard tests whether the sharp identification interval supports a point-error budget after scientific meaning is resolved.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Results.} A frozen public snapshot retains 12 TCGA cohorts, 30 genes, 6,210 specimens, and all 360 cohort--gene families. SQL and pandas counts agree on 5,760 specifications. Shared completions remove 3,048 independent-box alarms among 43,200 pairs, including 289 beyond exact equivalence merging. Uniform-intent clarification is 1.773 versus 1.833 questions for the equivalence-merged baseline (3.28\%); the per-family best fixed order matches the dynamic program. After all fields are selected, 720 TCGA specifications still fail a 2.5 pp point-error budget. KIRC--VHL cohort frequency spans 32.42--53.91\%, despite a fixed profiled fraction of 41.29\%. A separate MSK-IMPACT cohort adds 480 verified specifications and 562 repeated-specimen patients; all selected genes occur in both source panels. Prepared-table audits of the full TCGA registry take median 1.04--1.10 seconds in serial measurements; table preparation takes 20.56 seconds separately.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Interpretation.} Scientific clarification chooses a function; missing observations determine its shared input. CLARA separates semantic stability from point identification. A typed guard returns clarification, interval, or point outcomes with hash-bound, replayable provenance receipts. Forty-three software contracts, independent global TCGA optimization, 64 small-world fixtures, frozen inputs, full registries, and vector figure sources accompany the work. These are execution and mathematical checks; no language-model evaluation, researcher study, or clinical-benefit claim is made.
